# Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

**Money Heist** is a Spanish heist crime drama television series created by Álex Pina.

## 🧠 Plot Summary
A criminal mastermind known as *The Professor* recruits eight skilled thieves to carry out an ambitious plan — to rob the Royal Mint of Spain while taking hostages to manipulate the police.

## 🎭 Main Characters
- **The Professor** (Sergio Marquina) – the mastermind
- **Tokyo** – the narrator and a bold member of the crew
- **Berlin** – The Professor’s brother and second-in-command
- **Nairobi** – expert in quality control and printing money
- **Rio** – a young hacker

## 💡 Favorite Quote
> “The second any blood is shed, we’ll no longer be Robin Hood, but just a bunch of plain motherf***ing punks.”

## ⭐ Why I Like It
Money Heist keeps me on the edge of my seat with its suspense, clever twists, and emotional storytelling.
